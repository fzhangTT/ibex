M-2 is amended to the three rows and 55 items.
- Is the "no dedicated red for a missing writer" concession acceptable once the bus count rules exist? For the export FORMAT, yes: the count rules make req/gnt/rvalid self-checking and the remaining rows are consumed by the plan, whose fcov gate is the delayed red. For the SUNSET, no: the sunset removes a marker on the strength of "the build's writers emit this row", and the bus rules cover 6 of the 28 emitted rows. The proof the sunset needs is per row and has two halves, both cheap: (a) structural, in every run: each writer registers the (source, event) rows it will emit (register_row), the sink fatals at elaboration when an emitted row has no registered writer, and Runtime derives the emitted set from the canary run's header rows (what registered writers announce) instead of the yaml declaration, so a deleted writer changes the emitted set by itself; (b) behavioural, once: a row's items sunset only after an E line of that row exists in a retained run, kept as a per-row first-seen list the trace tool reads next to the emitted set. With (a) and (b) the concession stands and the sunset is sound; without them the emitted set is a declaration, and a declaration is what the sunset was designed not to trust.
