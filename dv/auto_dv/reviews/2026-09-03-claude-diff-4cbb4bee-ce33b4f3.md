[medium][dv/auto_dv/tb/gen_tb_pkg.sv:514] Stale bus-error announcements can legitimise an unrelated later DUT trap. `note()` records one word per granted request while `take()` consumes exactly one word per trap record; a misaligned access with both halves erroring (`dbus_err_half=both`) leaves one announcement in the 256-deep queue, and any later DUT trap on that word (spurious or otherwise) is armed and accepted. The scoreboard document states "matches either word" and "armed once" but not this leftover. Recommendation: consume both words of a spanning access in `take()`, and report `announced - taken` at end of run as an error when non-zero (a cheap referee that also catches an announced error the DUT never trapped on).
