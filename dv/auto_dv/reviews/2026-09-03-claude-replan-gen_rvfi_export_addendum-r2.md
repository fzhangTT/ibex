N2. MUT-G's independent-count check is not same-instant (lines 175-176, 250-253). `retired` is carried in the marker precisely because a Python read after the ack is late (lines 74-79, 90: "Python's own read ... is a `>=` check"). The grant counts get no such treatment: the marker (line 36) carries no grant count and the fixture compares the E-line grant count with `evt_ibus_grants` read from Python after the ack. Between the flush instant (after the NBA of posedge P) and Python's read lie at least one negedge, where the driver may grant, and the ack posedge; after EOT the Zc program spins (`j 1b`), so ibus grants continue. An exact equality goes red on a correct implementation; relaxing it to `>=` lets MUT-G's single dropped line pass. Remedy: `flush()` samples `evt_ibus_grants`/`evt_dbus_grants` into the marker (`ibus_grants=`, `dbus_grants=`) and `read()` compares the E gnt counts to them exactly, the same pattern as `retired`.

N3. The cycle-stamp source is not one counter (line 221: "the interface cycle counter every writer already has"; ordering claim lines 246-248). gen_rvfi_if.sv:24-25, gen_bus_if.sv:30-37 and gen_misc_if.sv:12-13 carry identical posedge-incremented counters with async reset; gen_bus_driver stamps its transactions from a LOCAL counter incremented at the negedge and reset in its loop (gen_agents_pkg.sv:182, 214, 217, 230, 247), which runs one ahead of the posedge counters; gen_ctrl_if.sv and gen_scrkey_if.sv have no cycle counter at all. If the bus driver stamps E lines from its local counter, every gnt line before a retirement carries `cycle+1` and the non-decreasing-cycle rule of line 89 fails on the first record; the ctrl and scrkey writers have no source. The plan must name the single stamp source (the posedge `cycle` of the writer's interface, or `gen_bridge_if.cycle_count`, all equal) and forbid local counters.
