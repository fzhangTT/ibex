# TB defect register - the team's own testbench, tests and harness

Defects in the verification environment, not in the DUT. Owner: dv-lead; rows are opened from a measured
failure and land with the fix that closes them. A defect here is a property of `dv/auto_dv/**`, never of the
Ibex core: DUT findings live in `dv/auto_dv/docs/gen_bug_log.md`, whose scope note points here.

Every row states what failed, the mechanism with a file:line citation, and the evidence that survives the fix.
That last part is a rule rather than a habit: a divergence usually stops reproducing once its defect is fixed,
so a row resting on the observed symptom goes stale and a reader a month later cannot tell what was wrong. The
mechanism is what a row is for.

One row, T4, is a CAPABILITY GAP rather than a behaviour that misbehaves: nothing is wrong with what the
environment does, and what is missing is stimulus it cannot produce at all. It is kept here because its
consequence is a property of `dv/auto_dv/**` and because a reader asking why five committed bins never fill
would otherwise find no answer anywhere. Such a row states its class in its first cell, opens from a measured
ABSENCE rather than a measured failure, and may carry no fix.
